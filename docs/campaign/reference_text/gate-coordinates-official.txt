Drone Racing Track
 Gate Layout & Coordinate Reference for Contestants
 Bird's-eye view of the track. The blue line is the racing line; numbered
 markers identify each gate. Grid squares are 16.4 ft.
Coordinate System
Track boundary: 85 ft wide (X) by 165 ft long (Y).
Origin (0, 0): Top-left corner of the track boundary, as
marked on the map.
X axis: Measured to the right, along the top edge (0 to 85
ft).
Y axis: Measured downward on the map, along the left
edge (0 to 165 ft).
Gate position: X and Y give the center of each gate
opening.
Rotation: Direction of flight through the gate, measured
clockwise from 0°. 0° = toward the top edge (decreasing
Y), 90° = right (+X), 180° = down (+Y), 270° = left (−X).
Race order: Gates are flown in numerical order, starting at
Gate 1.
