3. Physical Qualifier 	
3.1 Objective 
	
Validate autonomous drone flight capabilities through a timed obstacle course, qualifying teams based on 
navigation precision, gate passage count, and lap performance. 
3.2 Program  
	
On site teams will have dedicated slots every day to train the software performance in the real track	
	
Time Slots: Multiple defined operational slots per team per day. 
Timing Activation: Runs are classified and timed starting from the first timing line cross / gate trigger.

3.3 Qualification rules 
	
Ranking Criteria: Leaderboard ranking is strictly determined based on the timing results of valid, completed 
runs.  
In order a run being valid the drone must fly without any intervention of human pilots 
Run Requirements:  
• In order to complete a run, the teams must complete 2 laps of the track. 
 
Qualfications requirement: 
• Qualification & Scoring 
Evaluated by the best overall time recorded during the final two days 
• Primary Scoring:  
Ranked by the maximum number of successfully passed gates. 
• Tie-Breaker:  
Fastest elapsed time to the last validly crossed gate.

4. FAQ  
• What is our access level to Betaflight? 
You can configure rates, PIDs, filters, and telemetry via the Betaflight CLI. However, full reflashing of 
the flight controller is not allowed. 
 
• How do the Flight Controller (FC) and Nvidia Jetson communicate? 
They connect via UART using MSP. The interface transmits RC control signals (TX) and streams  
 
• What are the Jetson system specs, power modes, and privileges? 
You have full root access on the Jetson Orin NX. The Jetson operates in a 25W power mode, and a 
dedicated power supply will be provided in your workshop area. 
 
• How are camera frames timestamped? 
Frames are timestamped directly on the Jetson Orin NX using CLOCK_MONOTONIC. 
 
• Can we control camera settings and get intrinsic/extrinsic calibrations? 
You can control exposure and gain. Formal camera calibration matrices (intrinsics/extrinsics) will not be 
provided, but the physical position and orientation of the IMU relative to the drone will be supplied. 
 
• Will you provide goggles and a controller for flying the drone? 
A ground station and RC controller will be provided for each team when needed.   
 
• Can we adjust camera tilt during flight? 
Yes, pitch adjustments can be made during flight. 
 
• Are the control APIs in VQ2 based on the actual drone, or is there an additional transition layer? 
The APIs will differ from the physical drone setup. Full details on transition calls and timing will be 
provided in future documentation. 
 
• Can we issue an emergency shutdown? 
You have a manual override function via the RC controller that immediately supersedes autonomous 
functions at any time. 
 
• Can camera footage be transmitted mid-flight? 
Yes, the drone includes an onboard video transmitter (VTX). 
 
• Will teams receive training upon arrival? 
Yes, setup instructions and hands-on support for drone arming will be provided. Spectator access and 
future qualifier schedules will be communicated at a later date.

• What actions result in disqualification? 
Any non-compliance or action outside the official rulebook results in immediate disqualification. 
 
• Can we stand near the flight path with PPE? 
No, standing near the flight path is strictly prohibited regardless of PPE. 
 
• What happens if a drone gets damaged during flight? 
Teams are not financially liable for drone damage. All repairs, maintenance, and battery charging are 
managed on-site by the Neros repair team. Teams may not use personal battery chargers. 
 
• Will a course map or external tracking systems (like motion capture) be available? 
No external motion trackers or true-state tracking systems exist inside the building. Course map 
distribution details will be announced later. 
 
• How much flight time does each team get? 
Teams receive dedicated slots per day per team, alongside dedicated time in the training cage. Manual 
piloting is permitted. 
 
• Do teams share drones or data? 
No, data is not shared among teams. Each team is assigned a fleet of 4 dedicated drones.
